Welcome to Sai Priya Palace

ಸಾಯಿ ಪ್ರಿಯಾ ಪ್ಯಾಲೇಸ್‌ಗೆ ಸ್ವಾಗತ

Located right on the NH Service Road in M.K. Hubli, Hotel Sai Priya Palace has been serving travelers and locals with hearty, home-style cooking. Whether you're on a long highway drive or looking for a satisfying meal in town, we bring you comfort food made fresh, every single day.
